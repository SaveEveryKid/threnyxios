Refund Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Last updated 30 September 2026

The short version. Ask within 14 days and we'll refund Chimes you haven't spent and bundles you haven't used. If something you paid for never arrived, or a purchase was made without permission, we'll make it right. Once Chimes are spent or packs are opened, the purchase can't be refunded, except where the law says otherwise.

1. What you're buying

Purchases in THRENYX are digital content: Chimes (the Game's premium currency) and bundles such as the First Purchase Bundle. They are delivered to your account as soon as your payment is confirmed. See the Terms of Service for how Virtual Items work.

2. When we refund

SituationWhat we do
Unspent Chimes, requested within 14 days of purchaseFull refund. We remove that purchase's Chimes from your account.
Unused bundle (none of its packs opened), requested within 14 daysFull refund. We remove the bundle's contents.
Season Pass with none of its packs opened, requested within 14 daysFull refund. We remove the packs, and Ranked Voice ends.
Paid for but never arrived (Chimes or a bundle missing from your account)We deliver it, or refund it if you prefer, at any time.
Charged twice or charged the wrong amountRefund of the extra charge.
Unauthorised purchase (for example, by a child without permission), reported promptlyWe review it, and usually refund it once, removing whatever was bought.
The Game shuts downWe'll handle unspent Chimes as described in the Terms and as the law requires.

3. When we don't

  • Chimes that have been spent (for example, on packs), or bundles whose packs have been opened. Once content is used it can't be returned.
  • Pack results. Packs are random, and the odds are published in the Game before you open one.
  • Requests more than 14 days after purchase, except for the cases in section 2 that have no time limit.
  • Accounts closed for breaking the Terms of Service, except where the law requires otherwise.

A partial refund is possible: if you spent some of a purchase's Chimes, we can refund the unspent share at our discretion.

4. If you're in the EU or UK

You normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content, that right ends once delivery begins, if you agreed to immediate delivery and acknowledged you'd lose the right. The Shop asks you to confirm this before you pay. We still honour the 14-day refund for unspent Chimes and unused bundles described above. Nothing here limits your rights if something you bought is faulty or not as described.

5. How to ask

  1. Email info@threnyxtcg.com and tell us your username (write from the email on your account, if it has one).
  2. Include the date and amount of the purchase, or the receipt Stripe emailed you.
  3. We reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. A chargeback may lead us to remove the disputed items and pause the account until it's resolved.
