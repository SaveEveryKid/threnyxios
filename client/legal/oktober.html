Oktoberfest 2026 · Official Rules

Effective 1 October 2026

The short version. Free to enter: sign in and play ranked online matches in October. The 8 players with the most points play a finals ladder over Halloween weekend, whenever they're free. The highest finals rating wins The Eighth Bell, a one-of-one digital card. It's a contest of skill: no purchase is necessary, and buying anything does not improve your chances.

1. Organizer

Oktoberfest 2026 (the “Tournament”) is run by Off The Map, operator of THRENYX (the “Game”). Contact: info@threnyxtcg.com. These rules are part of the Game's Terms of Service.

2. Dates (US Central Time)

  • Qualifying: Thursday 1 October 2026, 12:00 am, to Friday 30 October 2026, 11:59 pm.
  • Finals: Saturday 31 October 2026, 12:00 am, to Sunday 1 November 2026, 11:59 pm.

The Game's servers keep the official time.

3. Who can enter

  • Anyone eligible to play under the Terms of Service (13 or older) with a signed-in account. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission.
  • One account per person. Off The Map and its staff and contractors are not eligible.
  • Void where prohibited by law.

There is no fee. You enter by playing a ranked online match during qualifying. No purchase is necessary, and purchases do not affect points, matchmaking or the result.

4. Qualifying

  • Every ranked online match scores: 3 points for a win, 1 point for a loss.
  • A win against an Echo (a real player's saved Octave played by the Game's AI) scores 1 point, up to 3 Echo points a day.
  • Matches against the same opponent score up to 3 times a day. Friendly (code) matches do not score.
  • Ranking: most points, then most wins, then whoever reached their total first.
  • When qualifying closes, the top 8 become the finalists.

4a. Oktoberfest Packs

Every entrant who reaches these qualifying point totals receives Oktoberfest 2026 Packs, once each: 10 points (1 pack), 25 points (1 pack), 50 points (2 packs), 100 points (3 packs), 7 packs in all. Each pack holds 5 foil cards: four Rare or better and one Epic or better, with the odds published in the Game. They are not sold, have no cash value, and are delivered to the account the next time the player opens the Game. Each pack also has a 4% chance of a bonus sixth card: one of five Oktoberfest Set Champions (Cassius Thorne, The Unrung, Hourbell, Vega Sorn, Caska Vell) in a cosmetic autumn version available only from these packs, favouring ones the player does not yet have.

5. Finals

  • Finalists play ranked matches against each other only, at any time during the finals. There are no scheduled match times.
  • Every finalist starts with a finals rating of 1000, adjusted after each finals match using the Elo system (K = 32).
  • Matches between the same two finalists count up to 4 times.
  • To be eligible to win, a finalist must complete at least 3 finals matches.
  • The winner is the eligible finalist with the highest finals rating when the finals close. Ties go to most finals wins, then the higher qualifying seed.
  • Matches follow the Game's normal rules, including move clocks, forfeits and the 60-second disconnect rule.

6. The prize

One winner receives The Eighth Bell, a one-of-one digital card added to their account, with the Tournament win recorded in its history. Only one copy will ever exist, and it will never be reprinted or sold. The winner may keep it or trade it to another player using the Game's own trading features, and its history, including this Tournament win, stays with the card. Like all virtual items, it is a licence under the Terms of Service: it has no cash value and may not be sold or exchanged for money, inside or outside the Game. It will be delivered within 14 days of the finals ending. If no finalist completes 3 finals matches, the prize goes to the finalist with the most finals wins, or, if none were played, the top qualifier.

Finalists: all 8 finalists receive the Oktoberfest Finalist card back, a cosmetic item with no cash value.

7. Fair play

Cheating, colluding to trade wins, using more than one account, or exploiting bugs disqualifies a player, and points earned that way are removed. Off The Map may review any match and decide on disqualification, and its decisions are final. If an outage or bug seriously disrupts the Tournament, Off The Map may extend or adjust it, and will announce any change in the Game.

8. Publicity and privacy

Display names, points, records and finals results appear on the in-game leaderboard. Off The Map may announce the winner's display name. Personal data is handled under the Privacy Policy.

9. General

Taking part means accepting these rules. They are governed by the laws of the State of Alabama, as set out in the Terms of Service. Questions: info@threnyxtcg.com.
