Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version. We collect only what the Game needs: a username and password to sign you in (an email only if you choose to give one), your game progress, what you do in online play and Alliances, and a record of purchases. Live voice chat is optional and never recorded; to use it we keep only your age band and whether you passed a card or ID check. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number. There are no ads, no tracking cookies, no analytics companies, and we never sell your data. You can ask us to see, fix or delete your data at any time.

1. Who is responsible

THRENYX is operated by Off The Map, who controls the personal data described here. Contact: info@threnyxtcg.com.

3. What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We never sell or rent your personal data, and we don't share it for others' marketing.
  • We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or card numbers.
  • We never record, store, transcribe or analyse voice chat, and we don't keep face or voice data. The ID check's face comparison happens at Stripe, under Stripe's policy.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services that run the Game, each under contract and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in, saved progress, online play and Alliances (database and servers).
  • Stripe: payment processing, the card check and the ID check (Stripe Identity). Stripe receives your payment details, email and, for the ID check, your ID and selfie directly, under Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the Game's website, and relays live voice chat (Cloudflare Realtime). The relay passes audio through as it happens and doesn't keep it; like any call, it sees your IP address.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the sign-in library to your browser, so it sees your IP address when it loads.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.

Other players see your display name, rating and record in online play, and your Alliance sees your name and role. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect players' safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the Game (and we'd tell you first).

5. Where it's stored

Our service providers may store and process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: as long as your account exists. Deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request.
  • Online match records: kept while your account exists, to support ratings and cheating reviews.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as tax and accounting law requires (usually up to 7 years), even after an account is deleted.
  • Hosting logs: kept by our providers for a short period, typically days to weeks.
  • Friends and trades: friendships until removed; friend messages up to 30 days; offers and posts while open and a short time after; completed trades while your account exists (a card's history also travels with the card).
  • Voice chat: the audio is never kept. Records of voice sessions and voice chat events: 90 days. Age band and check results: while your account exists. Blocks: until you unblock or delete your account. Reports: while open, then up to 1 year after they're closed.

7. Your choices and rights

Playing requires a free account, made with just a username and password (an email is optional). You can ask us to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you, or get a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete your account and its data (this permanently removes your collection and anything you bought);
  • object to or restrict how we use it.

Email info@threnyxtcg.com, from the address on your account if it has one, and tell us your username. We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, or California), you may have additional rights, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. We'll never treat you differently for using these rights.

Legal bases (EU/UK): running the Game you asked for (contract); keeping the Game secure and fair (legitimate interests); and meeting legal obligations such as tax records.

8. Children

The Game is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it. Players aged 13 to 17 should have a parent or guardian's permission to create an account, and must have it to make purchases. Voice chat is for adults only: anyone who enters a birth date under 18, or whose ID check shows they're under 18, can't use it.

9. Storage on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings in your browser's local storage, and, when you're signed in, keeps your sign-in session there. These are needed for the Game to work; we don't use tracking cookies. Clearing your browser's data for this site signs you out and removes any progress that isn't saved to an account.

10. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Our database only lets you read your own private data, and anything of value, such as matches and purchases, is handled only by our servers. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we'll tell you as the law requires.

11. Changes

We'll post updates here and change the date above. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the Game before it takes effect.
