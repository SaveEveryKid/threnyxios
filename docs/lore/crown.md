# THE ASHEN CROWN — Champion Backstories

> *"Duty outlives the realm."*
> Seat: **Cinderhold**, a fortress of black glass fused from the burning of Solenne. Custom: ritual mourning that has lasted 21 years. Every night the **Ashen Choir** reads the Roll of the Fallen aloud, and every morning the court wears fresh ash across the mouth: the **Mourner's Hand**.
> The crack: **Restorationists** (reclaim the Ashfields and the throne: by Clash if possible, by the Fork if not) against **Mourners** (the empire is dead; honour it and let it rest).

---

### Maelis Vantaine — *The Empress Who Burned* · Mythic
The last Empress of the Choir-Throne took her crown at nineteen and never once loosened her grip on it. She was brilliant, cold, and hated to be surprised, and in thirty years only one thing surprised her: her eldest son **Aldric** defecting to the twelfth city for love. (The world doesn't remember Aldric. Maelis is the only person who ever felt the gap.) When the War of the Last Verse turned against the Crown, Maelis ordered the Hourwrights to build the Stilling Fork. She meant to strike it herself and carry the guilt alone.
She vanished the night Solenne burned. Every account names her as the one who gave the order.
- **Wants:** that her children never learn what she knows.
- **Flaw:** believes guilt is something you can carry for other people.
- **Secret (SEALED):** she survived. She is the First Silence.
- **Arc cards:** *Maelis — The Coronation* · *Maelis — The Night of the Fork*.

### Aurelle Vantaine — *The Ash Regent* · Legendary
Maelis's second child (as far as anyone remembers, the eldest). She was trained by Sir Hadric Moor, loved Caska Vell at sixteen, and commanded the Crown's siege lines at seventeen. When the Recoil burned Solenne she was on a hill forty miles away, watching the twelfth city vanish. She came home to ash, a missing mother, and a court that needed someone to kneel to. She gave them a story (the twelfth city was building a weapon; we struck first) and has ruled on that story for twenty-one years.
- **Wants:** to reclaim the Ashfields by lawful Clash and end the mourning.
- **Fears:** the truth, which she knows and has never said aloud.
- **Secret (SEALED):** she struck the Fork, on a forged dispatch, before her mother arrived. She doesn't know it was forged. She has never forgiven herself, which is why she cannot forgive anyone else.
- **Relationships:** Hadric (father in all but blood) · Corvin (rival brother) · Caska (the life she didn't get) · the Hollow King (she erased him and doesn't know it).
- **At Firstfall:** files the Crown's Claim to Lirae, because if Lirae is ever *sung*, everyone remembers everything.
- **Arc cards:** *Aurelle — Uncrowned* · *Aurelle — The Confession* (Season 3+).

### Cassius Thorne — *Pyre-Marshal* · Legendary
The Pyre at Cinderhold burns every night. On it go slips of paper, each bearing the name of a Crown soldier who fell since the Unsinging. Cassius tends it. He was a quartermaster in the war, the man who counted the dead, and he came out of it convinced that the Crown's dead are a resource that is being wasted on grief. He is the heart of the **Restorationist** movement and Corvin's most important ally at court.
- **Wants:** the Fork rebuilt and struck again, at a city of the Crown's choosing, *"so that the last war is the last war."*
- **Flaw:** has confused arithmetic for morality.
- **Secret:** he has begun adding the names of the living to the Pyre, people who oppose him. None of them have died. Yet.

### Ser Brannoc — *The Unburnt* · Legendary
He was a knight of the palace guard on duty the night of the Recoil. When the fire rolled through Solenne he walked into it looking for the Empress, and three days later he walked out alone. His skin had cooled like glass: smooth, dark and faintly translucent over the veins. He doesn't speak of what he saw inside. He fights for the Regent, but he answers to no one and sleeps in the Ashfields rather than the fortress.
- **Wants:** to finish the search he started that night.
- **Secret:** inside the fire he saw a burned woman walking out of the palace with a bell in her hands. He let her go. He has never told Aurelle.
- **Why he never loses twice:** *"Fire teaches you that it always comes back."*

### Corvin Vantaine — *The Pale Prince* · Epic
Eight years old when Solenne burned. He was raised in mourning by a sister who was busy being a queen. He learned early that grief is a performance the court requires, and that he was the only one willing to admit it. He wears his mother's burned crown on a chain around his neck. Charming, funny, honest about his ambitions, and utterly ruthless.
- **Wants:** the empire back, by fear. The Fork is the only thing the world has ever truly obeyed.
- **Flaw:** he is right about his family's hypocrisy and wrong about almost everything else.
- **Secret:** he has been meeting Ottavio Brask. Brask has promised him the location of the third tine.
- **At Firstfall:** breaks the Accord in Chapter IV to seize Lirae as a power source for a new Fork.
- **Relationships:** Rhosyn (lover) · the Cinder Widow (his weapon; he lied to her) · Cassius (ally) · Aurelle (the sister he loves and will destroy).

### The Ashen Choir — *Mourners of Solenne* · Epic
Twelve veiled singers, their faces never shown, who read the Roll of the Fallen aloud every night. It takes four hours. They have not missed a single night in twenty-one years; when a member dies, another veiled figure takes their place before the next reading. Nobody outside the Choir knows who they are. Lady Seraphine keeps the Roll they read from.
- **Secret:** since Firstfall the Roll is **one name longer every night**, and none of them can pronounce the new names.

### Dame Orla Cinder — *Standard-Bearer* · Epic
On the night of the Recoil, Orla carried the Crown's battle standard out of the burning palace barracks with her gauntlets on fire, because the standard *must never touch the ground*. Her hands are scarred into claws; she carries the standard strapped to her back. Every Crown knight alive trained under her colours.
- **Wants:** one more charge. The Accord took war from her, and she hasn't forgiven it.
- **Relationship:** mentor rival to Hadric. They've argued about the correct way to hold a shield wall for forty years.

### Lysander Crane — *Master of Masks* · Epic
The Crown's spymaster, who wears a porcelain mask painted for whichever court he's visiting. No one living has seen his face; there are three competing rumours about why. He is Aurelle's most trusted advisor and Corvin's best-paid informant. He considers these two jobs complementary.
- **Wants:** to be on the winning side of the next war, whichever side that is.
- **Secret:** he knows about Aurelle's recurring dream of an older brother, and he sold it to Corvin, who didn't understand it. Lysander does.

### Lady Seraphine Ashgrove — *Keeper of Names* · Epic
Custodian of the Roll of the Fallen, the most complete record of the dead of Solenne, compiled from ration lists, family testimony and the Pyre. She is precise, kind and exhausted. Over the years she has found **eleven names in her own handwriting** that she doesn't remember writing. All eleven have the same family name, and it isn't a name anyone recognises.
- **Wants:** to know whose names they are.
- **Hook:** those eleven are the royal household of the twelfth city. She wrote them the night of the Unsinging, in the minute before she forgot them.

### Rhosyn Vale — *The Mourning Blade* · Rare
Aurelle's sworn sword and the finest duelist in Cinderhold. She fights with twin blades tuned a semitone apart, and the dissonance makes opponents flinch. She has been Corvin's lover for three years. She loves Aurelle as a sister-in-arms and Corvin as something much worse.
- **Wants:** not to have to choose.
- **At Firstfall:** she'll have to. Chapter IV: she rides with Corvin, and at the bridge she lowers one blade, but not the other.

### Isolde Marr — *The Cinder Widow* · Rare
A glassblower's wife whose husband died in the Recoil. Corvin found her at the Pyre six years ago and told her gently, with fabricated proof, that Vigil saboteurs had tampered with the Fork. She has killed eleven Vigil priests since. She paints her whole face with ash as a death mask and lights a candle for each kill.
- **Tragedy:** there was no sabotage. When she learns the truth, it won't be Corvin who pays first. It will be her own belief.
- **Arc card:** *Isolde — The Last Candle* (Redeemed).

### Captain Mira Voss — *Cinderhold Warden* · Rare
Commander of Cinderhold's single gate, which has never been breached in twenty-one years (nobody has tried, which she considers a victory). She is dry, practical and loyal to the office rather than the person. She is at her best when the enemy thinks they have the upper hand.

### Brother Edric Sallow — *Pyre-Priest* · Rare
He reads the future in the smoke of burning names. He predicted the Unended Night and became famous for it, then failed at every prediction after, and quietly began burning extra names to thicken the smoke. He now works for Cassius. He is starting to see things in the smoke that frighten him: a bell, a crown with an empty setting, and a star.

### Tobiah Marsh — *The Grey Herald* · Rare
By Accord law no one may strike a herald, so Tobiah has become a master of the most precise insult the law allows. He carries the Crown's challenges to Coda and has a surprising number of friends in every faction, most of whom he has also insulted.

### Bastian Crowe — *Ashfield Scavenger* · Rare
A deserter who ran from the siege lines before the Unsinging. He has spent twenty years digging relics out of the Ashfields and selling them to collectors in Carillon. Last month he dug up a long, thin piece of metal that **hums**. He hasn't sold it. He sleeps with it under his pillow, and every night he dreams of a tuning fork.
- **Hook:** it is **the third tine**.

### Hollis Brand — *Veteran of Solenne* · Rare
He was at the siege of the twelfth city. He remembers marching toward a city on a morning in spring, then nothing: three weeks of his life are simply missing. He wakes every night weeping for strangers. He joined the Mourners because they're the only people who don't ask him why.

### Sir Hadric Moor — *The Last Shield* · Common
Sixty-one. He trained Aurelle and Iver Hale, and buried every other knight he ever taught. His shield is studded with their melted badges. He is Aurelle's conscience and the one person at court who calls her *girl*. He hasn't said Iver's name in nineteen years.
- **At Firstfall:** Chapter V, he rides to Coda to stop Iver from arresting Corvin, and says his name.

### Tamsin Ashby — *Ember-Page* · Common
Twelve years old, an orphan of the Ashfields, and Hadric's squire. She carries the lantern holding the **last ember of Solenne**, a coal from the palace hearth that has never gone out (Nan keeps it fed when Tamsin sleeps). She is ferocious and takes every loss personally.

### Pip Harrow — *Ashguard Recruit* · Common
Born the year the Accord was signed, Pip has never known a war. He is desperate to prove he'd have been brave in one. He is always the first to volunteer for anything, which Orla says will either make him a hero or a name on the Roll.

### Old Nan Greaves — *Hearth-Keeper* · Common
She keeps the fires of Cinderhold burning and the Regent fed. She has outlived three husbands and two kings. She's the only person who refused Aurelle's order to burn the empty portrait (Chapter VII) and the only one who could get away with it.

### Garrick Slate — *Obsidian Mason* · Common
He built Cinderhold's walls from the black glass of Solenne, which means he has spent twenty years laying bricks made of his neighbours' homes. He talks to them while he works.

### Wynn Cole — *Mourning Drummer* · Common
Wynn beats the slow march at every Crown funeral. Lately the drum has been busier, and Wynn has started to worry about who's counting.

### Sera Flint — *Ember-Archer* · Common
An archer of the Cinderhold walls who waits for the moment her target gathers itself, because in that moment they aren't watching. Her motto is carved into her bow: *"You breathed."*

### Joss Tallow — *Candle-Thief* · Common
He steals candles from Vigil shrines and sells them back to the Vigil. He considers it the most honest trade in the Meridian, and is widely suspected of being the only person who has ever made both the Crown and the Vigil laugh.
