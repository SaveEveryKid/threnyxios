# THE HOURWRIGHTS — Champion Backstories

> *"Everything has a price. Especially time."*
> Seat: **Carillon**, a canal city of ten thousand bells, each tuned to a sold hour of someone's life. Custom: contracts for everything; tolling (hours drawn from a person's note and sealed in bells); automata powered by bought time.
> The crack: the **Guildmasters** (time is property) against **the Unwound** (freed hour-debtors who smash tolling bells and return the years).

---

### Ilsabet Brask — *Architect of the Fork* · Mythic
The greatest resonance engineer who ever lived: Ottavio's wife and Imre's mother. She designed the Stilling Fork to prove that any note could be cancelled cleanly, *without recoil*, as long as all three tines were struck together. She believed it would never be used. It was commissioned anyway. The night before it was struck, she was found dead in a locked bell tower in Carillon: no wounds, clocks stopped at 11:59, three footprints in the dust that faded after three steps.
- **What she was doing that night (SEALED):** she had discovered that her husband had removed the third tine to sell it, and was writing a letter to the Empress to warn her that without it the Fork would recoil. The letter was never delivered. Someone stopped the clocks.
- **Arc:** her design notes are the MacGuffin of Season 2.

### Ottavio Brask — *The Tollmaster* · Legendary
Guildmaster of Carillon for ninety years. He is 140 and looks 60, living on hours bought from the poor; eleven of them tick in his coat at any time. He is warm, generous, patient and a genuinely good grandfather to the Guild's apprentices. He is also the architect of the greatest crime in Threnyx.
- **What he did (SEALED):** forged the dispatch that made Aurelle strike the Fork early; removed the third tine to sell it (it would have made him the most powerful man alive); knowingly let Solenne burn.
- **Wants:** to own time itself. The Fork, rebuilt, would let him *cancel* a person's note and keep their remaining years.
- **Relationship:** he loved Ilsabet. He genuinely does not know who killed her, and is the only person in the story who would pay any price to find out.

### Hourbell — *The Colossus of Carillon* · Legendary
A bell-tower that walks. It was built during the war to defend the harbour and wound with ten thousand hours donated by the city's poor, who had no choice. It has stood motionless in the harbour for twenty years, waiting. Children dare each other to touch it. In Firstfall Week 8 it moves for the first time since the war: one step, toward the crater.

### Countess Amaranth Dray — *Buyer of Decades* · Legendary
She is two hundred years old and has never done anything herself. She buys the hours of great artists, generals and lovers and savours them like vintage wine, experiencing slivers of their lives. She is Brask's oldest client and his only equal. She is bored, and she has heard that a star has fallen: an hour of a star would be the finest vintage in history.

### Imre Vask — *The Unwound* · Epic
The Tollmaster's son, who took his dead mother's name at fifteen. Leader of **the Unwound**, freed debtors who smash tolling bells and give the stolen years back to their owners (it works, badly: the years come back as fever dreams). He fights like a man with nothing to save.
- **Wants:** his father in the Stillhouse on trial; his mother's death explained.
- **At Firstfall:** Chapter VIII, he finds his mother's drawings in his father's vault.

### Sabine Orrow — *Chronosurgeon* · Epic
She draws hours from the dying (with consent, usually) and gives them to sick children. Each surgery is both a mercy and a hanging offence under Guild law, because hours are *property*. She keeps Tock alive with hours she buys herself, because he once carried her out of a burning ward during the war.

### Foreman Gideon Brace — *Master of Engines* · Epic
The engineer who keeps Carillon's automata running, half of his body replaced by his own work. He knows every machine in the city by its tick. He believes the automata are becoming people. He has started quietly giving them names.

### Quenby Lathe — *Tinker-Oracle* · Epic
She built the **Oracle Engine**, a clockwork predictor of human choices fed by Maud Kessary's Ledger (which she has never seen, but reverse-engineered from public Clash results). It is right more often than she is. It has begun predicting *her*, and she has stopped sleeping.

### Lark Ferro — *Canal Runner* · Epic
A smuggler of tolled hours who runs the canals by night and has never been caught. Every job pays for the next one. He is secretly smuggling hours *back* to the families they were bought from, at a loss. Imre doesn't know. Lark likes it that way.

### Tock — *The Last Soldier* · Rare
Unit Seven of the Carillon Automaton Brigade, built for the War of the Last Verse. His unit was told to *hold the flooded square until relieved*. The war ended; nobody came. He is still there, twenty years later, holding position. Birds nest on his shoulders. He fights when the square is threatened, and in Clashes when Sabine asks.
- **Why he's heartbreaking:** his relief order was sent. It was addressed to a commander in the twelfth city's campaign, a name that no longer exists, so it was never delivered.

### Ada Kettleburn — *Boiler-Knight* · Rare
She wears armour driven by a boiler on her back. The more pressure she holds in, the harder she hits. She has exploded twice. She is the Guild's most decorated knight and its most expensive medical bill.

### The Twelve-Handed Clock — *Automaton Duelist* · Rare
A dueling automaton built for Brask's private entertainment. It has twelve arms and has never been told to stop. Gideon believes it wants to be. Brask believes it's worth more running.

### Silas Grint — *Loan-Shark* · Rare
He lends hours at terrible rates to people who have run out of time. Everyone in the canal quarter owes him something, including, it's rumoured, Lark Ferro and a very senior Arbiter.

### Felicity Brasswhistle — *Signal-Master* · Rare
She runs the semaphore towers of Carillon and can read intention from how a person stands. She's the Unwound's best-kept secret: every warning they've ever received came from her towers.

### Corwin Ashlock — *Escapement Duelist* · Rare
A dueling champion for hire who fights to the rhythm of a clock escapement: once started, never missing a beat. Undefeated in three-fight streaks; famously brittle after a loss.

### The Debt-Hounds — *Collectors' Pack* · Rare
Clockwork hounds that can smell the time a person has spent. They track every debtor in Carillon and never forget a scent. They were built with Ilsabet's resonance sensors. In Week 8 they all turn toward the crater at once and howl.

### Hesper Quill — *Debt-Collector* · Common
She collects unpaid hours for the Guild, with a clockwork jaw fixed in a permanent polite smile (the original was broken by a debtor). She is currently hunting Imre Vask, and she admires him.

### Pell & Pim — *The Bellwright Twins* · Common
Identical twin saboteurs in Brask's employ. They finish each other's sentences and each other's demolitions. One of them (which one is unclear) sabotaged the bell tower where Ilsabet died, the week before, on Brask's orders, *"to test its locks"*.

### Benn Oakes — *Tolled Laborer* · Common
He sold twenty years of his life to feed his family. He is thirty-one and looks sixty. He works the harbour cranes, and is very, very strong.

### Dilly Sprocket — *Apprentice* · Common
Ten years old, apprenticed to Foreman Brace. She has built her own automaton, which follows her everywhere and is extremely rude to adults.

### Sentry Unit Nine — *Clockwork Guard* · Common
One of hundreds of guard automata patrolling Carillon. Unit Nine has started pausing to watch the sunset. Nobody has reported it. Gideon knows, and has named it *Nine*.

### Odo Fennick — *Lamplighter* · Common
He lights the gas-lamps of Carillon every evening and knows everyone who walks the streets after dark, and what they're up to.

### Rue Marlow — *Canal Smuggler* · Common
She smuggles in a boat painted to look like it's sinking. It isn't sinking. Usually.

### Tomas Keel — *Bellwright Journeyman* · Common
He tunes the bells that hold other people's years, and he has started to hear voices in them. One of them, in the oldest bell in Brask's vault, keeps saying *"the clocks, stop the clocks"*. He hasn't told the Guild.
