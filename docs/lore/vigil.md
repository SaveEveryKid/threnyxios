# THE VIGIL — Champion Backstories

> *"Sing, so the Voice is not alone."*
> Seat: **Lanternfast**, a lighthouse-spiral city that hasn't been dark for 120 years, where the choir sings through every Hush.
> Custom: sleeplessness as devotion (eyes painted open on the lids); lanterns that are never allowed to go out; the Long Procession that walks the Meridian every night.
> The crack: the **Cantors** (end the Hush at any cost, even burning the Margin) against the **Lanterns** (protect people *from* the Hush, not the doctrine). Since the **Unended Night** (12 BA, when the prophesied end didn't come), the Cantors hold power.

---

### Lirae — *The Fallen Note* · Mythic
She walked out of the first star ever to fall. She looks like a girl of eighteen made of light that behaves like sound: her outline ripples like a plucked string. Her eyes are closed, and she has never opened them. She can hear what every person is about to do. She doesn't understand, yet, that people lie, or why anyone would want to. The Vigil found her camp second (after Vega and Maren) and dressed her in a choir robe too big for her.
- **What she is (SEALED):** the note of the twelfth city, which lost its anchor in the sky when the city was unsung and has been falling ever since. If she is fully *sung*, the city returns and everyone remembers everything.
- **What she wants:** to finish her note. She doesn't know what that means.
- **Relationships:** Maren (protector) · Caska (protector) · Juno (her first friend) · Nyx (the first person she remembered) · the Hollow King (she knows his name and won't say it until he's ready).

### High Cantor Absolom Reyne — *The Unsleeping Voice* · Legendary
Hero of the Unended Night. When the Vigil's prophecy failed and panic broke Lanternfast, Absolom climbed the tower and sang for **nine days without sleep** until the city calmed. He hasn't slept since. His voice is ruined; his faith is not. He believes the failed prophecy was a test, and that the Vigil failed it by being too gentle. He wants the Margin burned, because the Blank is the wound in the song.
- **Wants:** Lirae sung in Lanternfast, where he can control what she remembers.
- **Truth:** he's terrified. If Lirae proves the Vigil's cosmology wrong, the nine days meant nothing.

### Caska Vell — *Lantern-Knight* · Legendary
A lantern-knight whose flame has never gone out. She loved Aurelle Vantaine at sixteen, in the summer before the war, and wears a braided ash-grey cord Aurelle gave her. The war split them: the Vigil blamed the Crown for Solenne, and the Crown blamed the Vigil. She joined the Lanterns and has spent twenty years protecting people from the Hush instead of praying about it.
- **At Firstfall:** refuses Absolom's order to carry Lirae to Lanternfast (Chapter VI) and is stripped of her lantern. It's the first time the flame has gone out.
- **Arc card:** *Caska — Stripped of the Lamp* (Season Pass Alt Art) · *Caska — Relit* (Season 2).

### Saint Oriane — *The First Lamp* · Legendary
The founder of the Vigil, who stayed awake through the First Hush one hundred and twenty years ago and never slept again. She lived to a hundred and six without closing her eyes. Her lamp still burns at the top of Lanternfast. Some say she still tends it. The Sleepless Choir say they've seen her.
- **Mystery:** her tomb is empty. Her card depicts her as she was at thirty. Is it an Echo, a memory, or her?

### Maren Solt — *Hush-Hunter* · Epic
A mercenary who hunts the things that walk in the Hush: shapes made of absence that stand behind people at midnight. She has killed forty-one. She found Lirae second, looked at her for one long minute, and decided nobody was taking her anywhere. She hasn't been paid since.

### Cantor Evander Loom — *Choir-Master* · Epic
Master of Lanternfast's choir. He can hear a wrong note from across the city, and lately he's been hearing one in his High Cantor. He is the Vigil's quiet conscience and the likeliest person to break Absolom's hold on the faith.

### The Lantern Procession — *Pilgrims of the Long Night* · Epic
A procession of pilgrims who have walked the Meridian every night for sixty years, singing through the Hush. When one of them falls, the others sing louder. Old Teodor has walked with them for forty years.

### Aldo Brightwater — *Knight-Vigilant* · Epic
A knight who has stood the midnight watch at Lanternfast for eight years without sitting down once. The longer a fight goes on, the more awake he gets. He's cheerful about it, which unsettles everyone.

### Sister Morrow — *Hymn of Ash* · Epic
Absolom's most devoted Cantor. She sings the Burning Hymn, written by the Cantors to be sung while the Margin burns. She has been practising, and she is the one who strips Caska's lantern in Chapter VI.

### Marta Kell — *Vigilant* · Rare
A Vigil knight who can see in the Hush, a little. She is always first to the fight and last to leave. She's Caska's second, and she hasn't decided which side of the schism she's on.

### Tobin — *Censer-Bearer* · Rare
He swings the great censer at the head of every procession. It weighs as much as he does, and he has never once dropped it.

### The Sleepless Choir — *Midnight Voices* · Rare
The choir that sings through every Hush from the top of Lanternfast. Its members are chosen for voices that don't tire, and they are replaced often. Nobody asks what happens to the old ones.

### Sefa Dune — *Pilgrim-Seer* · Rare
A pilgrim from the far south who can hear the Threnody itself. She walked two thousand miles to hear Lirae, and says Lirae sounds like a lullaby she knew as a child but has never actually heard.
- **Hook:** Sefa's grandmother was a refugee from the twelfth city. The lullaby is its anthem.

### Zealot Horne — *Flame-Tongue* · Rare
A street-preacher who breathes fire at the end of every sermon. Nobody has asked him to stop, because nobody wants to be the one who asks.

### Juno — *Lamplighter* · Rare
A ten-year-old who carries a candle for Lirae everywhere. Lirae is the only person who has ever listened to her properly, and Juno would fight the world for her.

### Aldous Finch — *Bellringer* · Rare
The bellringer of Lanternfast, who rings the Hush-bell each night. He used to work in Carillon's bell-foundries, and knows exactly what's inside a bell when it rings. He won't ring a tolled bell. He has quietly replaced three.

### Brother Anselm — *The Sleepless* · Common
He hasn't slept in eleven years. He sees shapes in the corners of his eyes, and some of them are real. He is still gentle, and kind, and awake.

### Ione — *Choir-Child* · Common
Nine years old. She sings the midnight verse at Lanternfast, and her note holds longer than anyone's. Since Firstfall it has started holding *by itself*, after she stops singing. The Cantors are afraid of her.

### Lina Hart — *Novice* · Common
A new recruit who joined the day after Firstfall because she saw the star fall and couldn't think of anything else to do.

### Ebb — *Lantern-Squire* · Common
Caska's squire. He carries her spare lantern and her spare temper. When Caska's lantern is taken in Chapter VI, Ebb gives her his.

### Old Teodor — *Pilgrim* · Common
He has walked with the Procession for forty years. He has buried his wife, both his sons and his faith along the road, and he is still walking.

### Ottilie — *The Wakeful* · Common
A night-watch sentry at Lanternfast who stays awake by counting the stars. Since Firstfall she has counted one fewer every night. She has told no one, because she's afraid of what it means.

### Bess Tanner — *Choir-Warden* · Common
She keeps the choir children fed, dressed and in tune. She is terrifying, and every one of them would die for her.

### Tamar — *Hush-Scout* · Common
Maren's scout in the Hush, who moves without a sound. She has learned that the things in the dark only see what you expect them to.
