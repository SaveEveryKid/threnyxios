# THE THRENYX CODEX

> In-world documents, unlocked in the game's Codex through story chapters, Vault milestones and Legacy honors. They **disagree with each other on purpose**. The last section (SEALED) is for the studio only.

---

## I. Documents of the world

### 1. The Accord of the Octave (excerpt) — *signed at Coda, 0 AY*
> *I. There shall be no army raised in the Meridian, nor siege laid, nor city put to the torch.*
> *II. Every dispute between houses, guilds, faiths or peoples — of land, of debt, of blood, of succession, of any thing — shall be settled by Clash of Octaves, eight Champions to a side, the first to three victories prevailing.*
> *III. No Clash shall be fought in the Hush.*
> *IV. The Writ of a Champion binds them to fight for its holder. Writs may be sold, gifted, inherited, or won, and the history of a Writ shall be recorded and shall follow it.*
> *V. A thing that falls from the sky belongs to the Meridian, until claimed by Clash.*
> *VI. Those who break this Accord shall answer to the Quiet, who hear all and say nothing.*
> *Sealed by the hands of: the Regent of the Ashen Crown · the Guildmaster of Carillon · the High Cantor of the Vigil · the Clan-mothers of the Margin · the Duke of the Reach · the Tidewardens of the Stillwater · the Founder of the Quiet ·* **[eighth seal, unattributed]**

*(Clause V was written eighteen years before anything fell from the sky. Fans will ask why.)*

### 2. Proclamation of the Regent (Crown official history) — *1 AY*
> *Let it be known that the city of the North, whose name is struck from the rolls of the Meridian, did build in secret an engine to unsing Solenne; that the Crown, in defence of all peoples, did strike first; and that the fire which took our capital was the act of saboteurs within the Vigil, who have never answered for it.*

### 3. The Ledger Page (Hourwright, leaked) — *found in Scrawl, 14 AY*
> *Account 11-A. Commission: the Stilling Engine. Client: the Choir-Throne. Order to strike signed by H.I.M. Maelis Vantaine.* **Dated the 9th of Last Frost.**

*(Solenne burned on the 8th. The Empress vanished on the 8th.)*

### 4. The Sermon of the Wrong Note (Vigil) — *High Cantor Absolom, 5 AY*
> *The twelfth city sang a false note. We know this because the Voice removed it. The Fork was not a weapon; it was a tuning. The fire was the Voice's grief at having to do it.*

### 5. Testimony of Sister Smudge (Margin oral record)
> *They weren't taken. They left. The city sang its own name backwards and walked out of the song so the Empress could never have it. Only the King stayed, to remember. And then he forgot too, a little. That's what staying costs.*

### 6. A Prophecy of Grael (recorded by Wick, 13 AY)
> *"The ——— will fall and the ——— will remember. The ——— who struck it wears a ——— crown. Ask the ——— in the portrait."*

### 7. Practice Draft (Iris Vellum's private papers)
> *To the Commander of the Northern Lines, in the Crown cipher: the city will strike at dawn. Do not wait for Her Majesty. — [signature smudged]*
> *(In the margin, in Iris's hand: "Carillon paper. Client paid in bells.")*

### 8. The Roll of the Fallen, page 1,412 (Crown)
Eleven names in Lady Seraphine's handwriting that she doesn't remember writing, all sharing a family name nobody recognises. The page is water-stained, as if someone cried on it the night it was written.

### 9. Measurements of Castor Vey (Quiet, sealed)
> *Tines I and II resonate at 211.7 and 317.6. Their interval implies a third at 423.4. Something within two hundred miles of Solenne is still humming at 423.4.*

## IV. ◼ SEALED — studio truth (never shipped as text)

1. **The Vantaine children.** Maelis had five: **Aldric** (eldest; left for the twelfth city, became its king by marriage, unsung, now the Hollow King), **Aurelle**, **Corvin**, and the twins **Edda and Evrin**, who died in the Recoil. The portrait in the First Silence's room shows all five. The gallery at Cinderhold has an empty frame (Aldric's) and a black-veiled frame (the twins').
2. **The chain of guilt at the Unsinging:**
   - **Ottavio Brask** forged the dispatch (on Carillon paper, via Iris Vellum) to make the Crown fire early, and removed the third tine to sell it.
   - **Ilsabet Brask** discovered this and was writing to warn the Empress. She was killed in the locked tower that night. **The killer is Lysander Crane**, working for Brask at the time; he stopped the clocks to hide the hour. Brask ordered her *silenced* (bribed or held), not killed. Lysander decided otherwise. Brask doesn't know.
   - **Aurelle**, seventeen, received the forged dispatch and struck the Fork before her mother arrived.
   - Without the third tine, the Fork **recoiled** and burned Solenne, killing the twins.
   - **Maelis** walked out of the fire carrying the two surviving tines (Brannoc saw her), took a vow of silence, founded the Quiet and wrote the Accord.
3. **The eighth seal** was signed by Aldric, who walked into Coda as a half-unsung fragment on the day of the signing. Every signatory saw him and every signatory forgot. His seal is why Accord clause V exists: he wrote it, knowing his city's note was falling.
4. **The third tine** is under Bastian Crowe's pillow in the Ashfields.
5. **Lirae** is the twelfth city's note. Sung fully, the city returns, the Hush shrinks, and everyone remembers everything, including all of the above.
6. **Where it goes:** Season 2 *UNSUNG* (the Twelfth King; the third tine found) → Season 3 *THE CONFESSION* (Aurelle) → Season 4 *THE SINGING* (the city returns, or doesn't).
