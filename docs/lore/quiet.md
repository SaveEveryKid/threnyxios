# THE QUIET — Champion Backstories

> *"Judgment is silent."*
> Seat: **the Stillhouse** at Coda, an acoustically dead hall of black stone on the shore of the Stillwater. Custom: vows of silence, taken in stages (a year, a decade, a life); bells worn with their clappers removed. Only the Arbiter-Primus may speak, and only one word per Clash of record: the verdict.
> The crack: the Quiet are supposed to be neutral. Their leader is a Vantaine, and their vaults hold two-thirds of a doomsday weapon.

---

### The First Silence — *Judge of Coda* · Mythic
Founder and leader of the Quiet since the day the Accord was signed. Nobody has seen their face or heard their voice. They wear a hood of black lacquer shaped like a bell, their hands are burned and wrapped in linen, and they carry a judge's staff topped with two tuning-fork tines. The First Silence drafted the Accord in a single night, in a hand that three signatories later said *"looked familiar"*.
- **Wants:** that Lirae never be sung, because if she is, everyone will remember everything.
- **Secret (SEALED):** Maelis Vantaine, the vanished Empress, who walked out of the burning palace with the Fork's two surviving tines and built an order to guard them and herself.
- **Tells for fans to find:** the Vantaine sword-form in her staff work · she never judges a Clash between her own children, and always recuses by ringing the bell twice · the Accord's signature date is the day after she vanished.

### Maud Kessary — *Keeper of the Ledger* · Legendary
She has recorded every Clash of record for nineteen years in a ledger chained to her body. It's the most complete description of human behaviour ever written: who Guards when frightened, who Strikes when ahead, who READs too early. She is the First Silence's only confidant and the only person alive who knows who she is. She chooses, every day, not to say it.
- **Wants:** for her ledger to outlive her, and to never have to open the one page she has sealed with wax.
- **Arc:** she will be the first to break the Quiet's neutrality, not with a word but by opening a page.

### Oriel Dane — *Arbiter-Primus, the Scales* · Legendary
Second in the Quiet and the only member permitted to speak, one word per Clash: *"Mine,"* *"Yours,"* or, once in nineteen years, *"No."* She has been accused of favouring the Crown. The records are spotless. She is exceptionally good at making sure they are.
- **Secret:** she knows the First Silence's handwriting matches the Empress's. She decided long ago that the Accord matters more than the truth.

### The Unrung — *Silencer of the Vaults* · Legendary
The guardian of the vault that holds the two tines. It wears a bell cast without a mouth. No one knows if there is a person inside. Wherever it walks, Attunement stops working, songs die, and people forget what they were about to say. The Vigil call it an abomination. The Quiet call it *necessary*.
- **Hook:** it was built by Ilsabet Brask, not the Quiet, as a safety device for the Fork, and it has been waiting twenty-one years for the third tine.

### Iver Hale — *The Turned Knight* · Epic
Hadric's second-best student, Aurelle's sword-brother at the siege. After the Unsinging he rode to Coda and joined the Quiet, and has never said why. The Crown calls him a traitor; the Quiet call him their finest Silencer. He has judged nineteen years of Crown Clashes without a single appeal.
- **Why he left (for Season 2):** he was on the hill with Aurelle and saw her strike the Fork. He couldn't serve her, and couldn't betray her. So he took a vow that meant he'd never have to say it.
- **At Firstfall:** chosen to arrest Corvin (Chapter V). Hadric stands in his way.

### Arbiter Senna Loe — *The Fair Hand* · Epic
The youngest Arbiter in the Quiet's history, and the only one who still believes the Accord is fair. She was a Coda street orphan whom the Quiet took in, and the law is the only family she's had. In Week 3 of Firstfall she reads a sealed verdict she wasn't meant to see: an order, in the First Silence's hand, to *"silence the fallen star by any means"*.
- **Arc:** idealist → whistleblower → *Senna — The Broken Seal* (Season 2).

### Castor Vey — *Warden of the Tines* · Epic
He guards the tines in the Stillhouse vaults and has quietly measured them for years. From their harmonics he has calculated that the missing third tine is **still intact, and somewhere within two hundred miles of Solenne**. He intends to find it first, and doesn't intend to tell anyone.

### Sable — *The Blind Duelist* · Epic
A Quiet novice who put out her own eyes to keep her vow more completely than anyone before her. She fights by listening to the silence around a blade. You cannot READ what she will do because she doesn't decide until the blade is already moving.

### Nils Arrow — *The Listener* · Epic
A farm boy who can hear a person decide, three heartbeats before they act. The Quiet recruited him at nine. When he guesses wrong, somehow nothing bad happens to him, as if the world forgives him for listening. The Arbiters have started to wonder if Nils is partly unsung.

### Ingrid Solm — *Silent Sister* · Rare
A sister who hasn't spoken in thirty years. She keeps a list of every lie she has heard, alphabetised, in her head. She has been listening to Lirae's humming from outside the Vigil camp and has started a new list.

### Veyra Knell — *Bell-Breaker* · Rare
She breaks the bells of Accord-breakers before their trials, so they enter the Stillhouse already silent. She believes silence is cleansing. She has broken more bells than anyone in history, and hopes to break Corvin's.

### Doran Pike — *Stillhouse Guard* · Rare
Twenty years at the Stillhouse doors without a missed watch. He lets the Clashes happen; he does not let anything else happen. He is married to Hester Vane and communicates with her entirely through the colour of the wax on his sealed lunches.

### Marek Soll — *Clerk of Seals* · Rare
He stamps every sealed verdict in Coda and remembers what was under the wax. He sells that memory, carefully, to the highest bidder. His best customer for three years has been Lysander Crane.

### Tessaly Mourne — *Hush-Warden* · Rare
She stands watch over Coda during the Hush, when nothing can be heard and anything can happen. She has learned patience that borders on stillness, and saves herself for the moment that matters.
- **At Firstfall:** she is the first to report that the Hush is lengthening faster than the Vigil's charts predict.

### Hob — *The Keyholder* · Rare
A cheerful little man with a ring of four hundred keys who opens every door in the Stillhouse except one. The Quiet have never asked him about that door. He hums in the corridors, which is technically forbidden, and nobody has ever reported him.
- **Hook:** the door is the First Silence's private room. Inside is a portrait of five children. Hob has seen it. He counted five.

### Lucan Grey — *Oathtaker* · Rare
A former Hourwright debt-collector who took the Quiet's vow to escape his debts, since the law doesn't let the Guild collect from the Silent. He administers the oath to every new Silencer. His hands shake when he does it, and every Silencer remembers.

### Ossian Drae — *The Tongueless* · Common
The Quiet's executioner cut out his own tongue to take the vow. He punishes Accord-breakers, and he enjoys it more than a servant of the law should. The First Silence keeps him because an order of judges needs someone who doesn't hesitate.

### Wren — *Bell-Runner* · Common
An eleven-year-old courier who carries sealed verdicts across Coda. She reads every one of them, using a trick with a hot knife she learned from Marek Soll. She hasn't told anyone what they say. Yet. She is the only other person who has read Senna's forbidden verdict.

### Hester Vane — *Wax-Sealer* · Common
She seals every document the Quiet produce and keeps a drop of wax from each in a jar taller than she is. The jar is, in fact, the only complete index of the Quiet's secrets. She hasn't realised this. Marek has.

### Dunstan Rook — *Gatekeeper* · Common
Keeper of Coda's landward gate. He has turned away two princes, a guildmaster and a saint, and was polite to all of them.

### Novice Aria — *Silent Student* · Common
In her first week of silence. She is terrible at it, apologises constantly under her breath, and is the best natural listener the Quiet have seen since Nils.

### Tallis Fenn — *Verdict-Runner* · Common
The fastest runner in Coda. He delivers verdicts before the loser has finished losing and is thrilled about it every single time.

### Gorm — *The Bell-Mute* · Common
A huge, gentle-looking Silencer who carries the Quiet's broken bells to be melted. Nobody knows if he took the vow or never learned to speak. Children follow him through the streets. He lets them.

### Osric Dunne — *Archivist* · Common
Keeper of the lower archive, where the Quiet file what no one wants read. He files very slowly and reads very quickly. He is the only person who has noticed that the Accord's eighth seal was **added to the document after the other seven dried**.
