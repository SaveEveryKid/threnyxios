# THE MARGIN — Champion Backstories

> *"What is forgotten is free."*
> Home: the ragged settlements along the edge of **the Blank**: **Scrawl**, **Rulings**, **Last Legible**. Scaffold-towns over white fog, lit by violet lamps (the only light that works near the Blank).
> Custom: no family names (names can be stolen); maps are sacred and illegal; people who were half-erased are treated as holy, or as warnings.
> The crack: remember the lost city, or stay free of it?

---

### The Hollow King — *Unsung* · Mythic
He walked out of the Blank eleven years ago with half his body missing: not wounded but *unwritten*, rendering as blank ruled paper. He had a crown with an empty setting, a coat in colours no one could name, and a single certainty: *I was a king, and someone took my name.* The Margin made him their king within a year. The Crown calls him a monster from the erased city who wants revenge. He does. He also wants something he can't describe: to be *called* by someone.
- **Truth (SEALED):** Aldric Vantaine, eldest son of the Empress, who left Solenne for love of a woman of the twelfth city and became its king by marriage. His wife, his children, his city and his name were unsung. He survived as a fragment because he was *half* of Solenne. His mother's blood anchored him.
- **Wants:** Lirae sung, whatever it costs the world.
- **The empty setting:** it held the twelfth city's crown-jewel, a tuning stone that sang the city's pitch. It fell into the Blank with everything else.
- **Arc cards:** *The Twelfth King* (redacted name) · *Aldric* (the name returned).

### Vega Sorn — *Cartographer of Nowhere* · Legendary
A former Quiet surveyor who went into the Blank on a rope to map its edge and came back with a map of streets that aren't there: streets that are *being rebuilt by no one*, a little more each month. She has mapped the Blank for eight years and is the only living person to have reached its centre and returned. Her sister Nyx went in after her once, and came back wrong.
- **Ritual:** every hour, on the hour, she writes her sister's name in a notebook. If she stops, she'll forget Nyx, like everyone else has.
- **At Firstfall:** first to reach the crater (Chapter II).

### Mother Ink — *Namethief* · Legendary
The oldest tattooist in Scrawl, who writes names onto the nameless: half-erased people who have forgotten their own. The names she gives are real. She *steals* them from the living, who then begin, slowly, to forget themselves. Bottles on her shelves hold violet wisps: names in storage. The name on her own arm changes every week.
- **Secret:** she was a scribe of the twelfth city. She survived because she was writing its name at the moment it was unsung, and the ink kept a little of her. She has been trying to rewrite the city ever since, one stolen name at a time.
- **Villain or saviour:** both. Fans will argue.

### Quill — *The Rewriter* · Legendary
A Margin scribe who has infiltrated the Quiet's archives for years, editing the records of Clashes the Margin lost. The losses stop having happened. The Quiet haven't noticed, because Quill edited that too. Quill might be one person, or several people sharing a name, or a story the Margin tells itself.
- **Hook:** Osric Dunne has noticed. He's been editing Quill's edits back. Neither knows who the other is.

### Nyx — *The Unremembered* · Epic
Vega's younger sister walked into the Blank to bring Vega home and came back impossible to remember: you forget her face within minutes, and her name within an hour. It makes her the best thief and assassin in the Meridian and the loneliest person in it. Only Vega remembers her, and only because of the notebook.
- **Wants:** one person, other than her sister, to remember her unprompted.
- **At Firstfall:** Lirae does. That changes everything for Nyx.
- **Arc card:** *Nyx — Remembered* (Season 2).

### Thessaly Pale — *Edge-Walker* · Epic
She walks the exact border of the Blank, where the world is half-remembered, and smuggles people across who need to disappear: debtors from Carillon, deserters from the Crown, heretics from Lanternfast. She always steps where you were going to step.

### Lorne Asher — *Blank-Diver* · Epic
He dives into the Blank on a rope for salvage, forgetting a little more of himself each time. He has forgotten how to be afraid, which is useful, and his mother's face, which is not. He fights best with nothing left to lose because, increasingly, he doesn't.

### Kestrel — *Fog-Runner* · Epic
A courier who runs messages through the fog, never taking the same path twice, because the Blank remembers paths and closes them. Kestrel carried Vega's first message out of the crater: *There is a girl here. She is singing.*

### The Paper Knight — *Remnant of the Twelfth* · Epic
A suit of armour made entirely of folded paper, patrolling the edge of the Blank. Inside is either a knight of the lost city or the memory of one. It won't remove its helm. It kneels to the Hollow King and to no one else, and it's the only thing Dask Holloway is afraid of, because he recognises how it holds its sword.

### Iris Vellum — *Forger* · Rare
The finest forger in the Meridian; the Quiet have enforced several of her verdicts. Twenty-one years ago, a gentleman from Carillon commissioned her to forge **a battlefield dispatch in the Crown's cipher**. She never met him again. She doesn't know what the dispatch said. She has kept the practice draft.
- **Hook:** the draft is evidence that the dispatch that made Aurelle strike the Fork was forged, and that it came from Carillon.

### Brother Blot — *The Smudged* · Rare
A former Vigil monk whose features blurred after years near the Blank. He preaches that being forgotten is a holy state, the soul freed from the song's judgment. Nobody remembers his sermons, which he takes as proof.

### Sorrel — *The Salvage-Queen* · Rare
She rules Scrawl's scrap-markets and builds wonders from things nobody remembers the use of. She is the Hollow King's quartermaster and the Margin's most practical mind.

### Tamsk — *Hollow Guard* · Rare
Captain of the Hollow King's bodyguard, recruited from men and women half-erased by the Blank. They don't remember their pasts, only their oath to him, which they've decided is enough.

### Wick — *Lamplighter of Scrawl* · Rare
An orphan who lights the violet lamps of Scrawl. She sees everyone who comes and goes and writes it all down, because Vega taught her the notebook trick. She's the reason the Margin always knows who's coming.

### Ivo Marrow — *Name-Seller* · Rare
He sells new identities to people fleeing their pasts, some forged by Iris, some stolen from Mother Ink's shelves. The customer is never told which.

### Lenka — *Ghostwright* · Rare
She finds people who have been half-forgotten and remembers them back into the world, one detail at a time. It costs her a detail of herself each time. She has forgotten the colour of her own eyes.

### Dask Holloway — *Remnant Knight* · Common
The only knight of the twelfth city who survived, because he was outside the walls on patrol that morning. He remembers nothing of the city, only his oath to a king whose name he can't recall. When the Hollow King walked out of the Blank, Dask knelt without knowing why. In Chapter IV he holds a bridge alone against Corvin's riders.

### Grael — *Scavenger-Prophet* · Common
Half-unsung, his prophecies come out with words missing. *"The ——— will fall and the ——— will remember."* He said it for six years before Firstfall.

### Mote — *Scrawl Urchin* · Common
A nine-year-old pickpocket who can walk into the Blank and come back remembering everything. She doesn't know this is supposed to be impossible. Vega does, and is quietly terrified by what it means.

### Fenwick Ashlar — *Blank-Surveyor* · Common
Vega's long-suffering assistant. He carries the instruments and does the arithmetic. He is quietly in love with a woman he can't remember the face of (Nyx), and keeps finding love letters in his own handwriting that he doesn't remember writing.

### Corbel — *Scaffold-Climber* · Common
He builds the scaffold-towns over the fog. He has fallen off every one of them and climbed back up every time.

### Sister Smudge — *Keeper of the Unnamed* · Common
She tends a graveyard of unmarked stones, one for every person lost with the twelfth city (she guesses at the number). She talks to them. Since Firstfall, she says they have started answering.

### The Inklings — *Drips of the Blank* · Common
Small inky things that drip from the edges of the Blank and pool into shapes, imitating whatever they see. Nobody knows if they're alive. Mote keeps one as a pet.

### Verity Crook — *Map-Thief* · Common
She steals Vega's maps and sells copies to people who want to get lost on purpose. Vega knows, and lets her, because every copy sold is another person who might remember a street.
