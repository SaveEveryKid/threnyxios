# PHASE 4 — CHARACTER BIBLE (FIRSTFALL: THE FIRST 30)

> Status: **LOCKED** (gate at bottom).
> **Superseded in part:** the set grew from 30 to 120 Champions. Rarities, stats and abilities are authoritative in `docs/05-card-catalog.md` (generated from the engine). Full backstories for all 120 are in `docs/lore/`. The flagship dossiers below remain canon.
> Test for every Champion: *would someone care about this character if the card had average stats?* If not, they were reworked before they reached this page.

## 1. Deck-building layers

| Layer | Values at launch | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Faction** | Ashen Crown · Quiet · Hourwrights · Margin · Vigil | Political home; faction-count synergies |
| **Class** | **Duelist** (momentum, pressure) · **Sentinel** (protection, Initiative) · **Trickster** (bluff, anti-READ) · **Seer** (information, READ) · **Artificer** (Focus) · **Martyr** (the Spent, revenge) | *How* a Champion wins — never *which Order* it uses |
| **Alignment** | Heroic · Villainous · Rogue · Corrupted · Redeemed | Hero/villain energy; alignment-count synergies |
| **Traits** | Vantaine · Knight · Child · Machine · Unsung · Sleepless · Silencer · Tolled · Royal · Assassin | Cross-faction synergy hooks |
| **Bonds** | Printed relationships between two named Champions | "Wait a minute" discoveries. Always written on the card — no secret mechanics |

**Launch alignment mix** (30): Heroic 13 · Villainous 8 · Rogue 6 · Corrupted 2 · Redeemed 1 ≈ 70% clear hero/villain, 30% complicated. On purpose.

Five relationships carry the whole franchise:

1. **Aurelle and the Hollow King.** She erased him and doesn't know it. He remembers that someone did. (SEALED)
2. **Aurelle and the First Silence.** A daughter hiding a crime from a mother who is hiding herself. (SEALED)
3. **Aurelle and Caska.** Lovers at sixteen. The war split them. Now they lead factions on opposite sides of the Firstfall Claims.
4. **Ottavio and Imre.** Father sells time; son frees the people he sold it from. Neither will lose to the other.
5. **Vega and Nyx.** Nyx walked into the Blank and came back impossible to remember. Vega writes her sister down every hour so she doesn't forget her.

All five exist without the player. The player walks into the middle of them.

## 3. Flagship dossiers

### AURELLE VANTAINE — *The Ash Regent*
**Crown · Sentinel · Heroic · Vantaine, Royal · LEGENDARY**
- **Look:** 38, severe and beautiful. A black-glass crown that is visibly **cracked** and bound with silver wire. A white mourning veil pushed back over one shoulder. Ash handprint across her mouth — the Mourner's Hand, worn by the Crown, but hers is always fresh. Carries a tuned greatsword that hums a low note. **Motif:** the cracked crown.
- **Wants:** to reclaim the Ashfields and end her people's twenty-one-year mourning.
- **Strength:** absolute discipline. She has never lost a Clash she chose to fight.
- **Flaw:** she cannot forgive, because forgiving others would mean admitting she needs forgiveness.
- **Worldview:** "A crown is a promise you keep when everyone who heard it is dead."
- **Secret:** she struck the Stilling Fork. (SEALED)
- **Enemy:** Corvin (politically), the Hollow King (publicly). **Ally:** Sir Hadric.
- **Gameplay:** Crown anchor. Gets stronger for every fallen ally: the Spent as her strength.
- **Narrative purpose:** the franchise's tragic protagonist. Heroic on the card; players will spend three seasons deciding if she deserves it.
- **Future arc cards:** *Aurelle — the Girl at the Fork* (17, the night of the Unsinging) · *Aurelle — Uncrowned* · *Aurelle — the Confession*.

### THE HOLLOW KING
**Margin · Trickster · Villainous · Unsung, Royal · MYTHIC**
- **Look:** a tall king whose left half **renders as blank paper** — crisp, clean, with faint ruled lines, like a page nobody wrote on. A crown with an **empty setting** where a jewel was. A long coat of the twelfth city's colours (which nobody can name). His shadow falls the wrong way. **Motif:** the empty setting.
- **Wants:** his name.
- **Strength:** he cannot be predicted, because he doesn't know who he is either.
- **Flaw:** he'd let the world end if it meant being remembered.
- **Worldview:** "You burned my city and forgot it. I am what forgetting costs."
- **Secret:** he is Aldric Vantaine, Aurelle's older brother. (SEALED)
- **Enemy:** the Crown. **Ally:** Dask Holloway, his last knight.
- **Gameplay:** the defining bluff card. READs against him fail. He wins by playing the Order you're sure he won't.
- **Narrative purpose:** the franchise's villain on first contact and its most tragic figure on the tenth. Every Crown loss screen is propaganda about him.
- **Future arc cards:** *The Twelfth King* (before the Unsinging, named — with the name **redacted on the card itself**) · *Aldric* (the name, returned).

### LIRAE — *The Fallen Note*
**Vigil · Seer · Heroic · Unsung · MYTHIC**
- **Look:** a young woman made of **light that behaves like sound** — her outline ripples in visible waves when she moves, like a plucked string. Bare feet that never quite touch the ground. Vigil lantern-bearers have dressed her in a choir-robe that's too big for her. Her eyes are closed; there is a faint pitch-glow behind the lids. **Motif:** concentric rings, like sound from a struck bell.
- **Wants:** to finish her note. She doesn't know what that means.
- **Strength:** she hears the next note — every person's.
- **Flaw:** she doesn't understand that people lie.
- **Worldview:** "Everyone is singing something. Most of you are singing that you're afraid."
- **Secret:** she is the twelfth city's note. (SEALED) If she is sung, everyone remembers.
- **Gameplay:** the READ Mythic. Her READs refund themselves.
- **Narrative purpose:** Season 1's MacGuffin, who turns out to be a person with her own will.

### THE FIRST SILENCE
**Quiet · Seer · Rogue · Silencer · LEGENDARY**
- **Look:** a robed figure under a **bell-shaped hood of black lacquer**, clapper removed, face never shown. Burned hands wrapped in white linen. Carries the judge's staff of Coda: a rod with two tuning-fork tines. **Motif:** the silent bell.
- **Wants:** that Lirae never be sung.
- **Strength:** has watched every Clash of consequence for nineteen years. Knows everyone's tells.
- **Flaw:** confuses silence with atonement.
- **Worldview:** (never speaks) — the Quiet's charter: *"There is a rest written in the score. We are its keepers."*
- **Secret:** she is Empress Maelis Vantaine. (SEALED)
- **Gameplay:** information control. Denies enemy READ and reveals the enemy hand.
- **Narrative purpose:** the franchise's ultimate reveal, seeded from the first day through burned hands and a Vantaine sword-form.

### CORVIN VANTAINE — *The Pale Prince*
**Crown · Duelist · Villainous · Vantaine, Royal · EPIC**
- **Look:** 29, bone-pale, elegant, **wears his mother's burned crown** (recovered from the Ashfields) around his neck on a chain. Fencing blacks, ember-red lining. Smiles too much. **Motif:** the crown worn as a pendant.
- **Wants:** to rebuild the Stilling Fork and restore the empire by fear.
- **Flaw:** he believes he's the only honest Vantaine — and he's right, which is what makes him dangerous.
- **Secret:** he has been meeting Ottavio Brask. Brask has offered him the location of the third tine.
- **Gameplay:** momentum — each win makes him harder to stop.

### OTTAVIO BRASK — *The Tollmaster*
**Hourwrights · Artificer · Villainous · Tolled · LEGENDARY**
- **Look:** heavy, warm, grandfatherly — until you see **eleven clock-faces set into his coat**, each ticking at a different speed: hours he has bought and is living on. He has lived 140 years and looks 60. **Motif:** the coat of clocks.
- **Wants:** to own time itself.
- **Strength:** patience measured in centuries.
- **Flaw:** he genuinely loved his wife and has never mourned her, because mourning is unproductive.
- **Secret:** he forged the dispatch that made Aurelle strike the Fork, and stole the third tine. (SEALED)
- **Gameplay:** Focus banker. Drains enemy Focus, feeds his own.

### IMRE VASK — *The Unwound*
**Hourwrights · Artificer · Heroic · Tolled · EPIC**
- **Look:** a young mechanic with **a tolling-needle scar on each forearm** and a sledgehammer made from a melted-down tolling bell. Took his mother's surname. **Motif:** the cracked bell hammer.
- **Wants:** to smash every tolling bell in Carillon and give the years back.
- **Flaw:** reckless; will spend everything on one blow.
- **Gameplay:** all-in Focus burst.

### CASKA VELL — *Lantern-Knight*
**Vigil · Sentinel · Heroic · Knight, Sleepless · EPIC**
- **Look:** a big, calm knight in indigo plate with **a lantern-halberd** whose flame has never gone out. **Eyes painted open** on her eyelids (Vigil sleepless rite). A braided ash-grey cord around her wrist — Aurelle gave it to her at sixteen. **Motif:** the lantern that never goes out.
- **Wants:** to protect Lirae from *everyone*, including her own High Cantor.
- **Flaw:** loyalty to people over orders, which makes her a traitor twice over.
- **Bond:** Aurelle Vantaine.
- **Gameplay:** the Vigil wall — sustain, Initiative, never-dies energy.

### VEGA SORN — *Cartographer of Nowhere*
**Margin · Seer · Heroic · — · EPIC**
- **Look:** a wiry surveyor covered in **map tattoos of streets that don't exist**, many still being inked. Brass surveying lenses, a satchel of scroll-maps, a pen-knife. **Motif:** the ink-wet map.
- **Wants:** to map the Blank before it finishes rebuilding itself.
- **Flaw:** she trusts paper more than memory, including her own.
- **Bond:** Nyx — "writes her sister down every hour."
- **Gameplay:** information. She learns the enemy hand.

### NYX — *The Unremembered*
**Margin · Trickster · Rogue · Unsung, Assassin · RARE**
- **Look:** you can't quite see her face — the art renders it as **an ink smear that follows her** like a motion blur. Hooded, fast, knives. **Motif:** the smeared face.
- **Wants:** someone other than her sister to remember her.
- **Gameplay:** anti-READ trickster. The card that makes "I READ you" wrong.

## 4. The rest of the thirty

**ASHEN CROWN**
- **Sir Hadric Moor — The Last Shield** · Sentinel · Heroic · Knight · COMMON. Sixty-one. Taught Aurelle and Iver to fight. Shield studded with the melted badges of every knight he outlived. *Bond: Aurelle.* Wants one more good war to die in, and the Accord took it from him.
- **Tamsin Ashby — Ember-Page** · Martyr · Heroic · Child · COMMON. Twelve. Carries Solenne's last ember in a lantern. Hadric's squire. Takes every loss personally; gets fiercer for it.
- **Rhosyn Vale — The Mourning Blade** · Duelist · Rogue · Knight · RARE. Aurelle's sworn sword. Corvin's secret lover. Fights with two swords tuned a semitone apart, which makes a sound people can't stand.
- **Isolde Marr — The Cinder Widow** · Martyr · Villainous · Assassin · RARE. Lost her husband in Solenne. Corvin told her the Vigil did it. She's been killing Vigil priests for six years. Veil of black lace, face ash-painted *entirely* white.

**THE QUIET**
- **Maud Kessary — Keeper of the Ledger** · Seer · Rogue · Silencer · EPIC. Records every Clash of record in a book chained to her body. Knows the First Silence's secret. Chooses, every day, not to tell.
- **Arbiter Senna Loe — The Fair Hand** · Sentinel · Heroic · Silencer · RARE. The youngest Arbiter. Believes the Accord is the best thing that ever happened. Is starting to find out what it's built on.
- **Iver Hale — The Turned Knight** · Sentinel · Redeemed · Knight, Silencer · RARE. Crown knight who deserted after the Unsinging, joined the Quiet, and has judged his old order's Clashes for nineteen years. Hadric has never spoken his name since.
- **Ossian Drae — The Tongueless** · Duelist · Villainous · Silencer · COMMON. The Quiet's executioner. Cut out his own tongue to take the vow. Enjoys the work.
- **Wren — Bell-Runner** · Seer · Heroic · Child · COMMON. Street child who carries sealed verdicts across Coda. Reads every seal. Tells no one. Yet.

**THE HOURWRIGHTS**
- **Sabine Orrow — Chronosurgeon** · Artificer · Rogue · Tolled · RARE. Tolls the dying to give years to sick children. Every one of her surgeries is both a crime and a mercy.
- **Tock — The Last Soldier** · Sentinel · Heroic · Machine · RARE. Clockwork soldier built for the war, still waiting for orders from an army that no longer exists. Runs on hours Sabine buys for him. *Bond: Sabine Orrow.*
- **Hesper Quill — Debt-Collector** · Duelist · Villainous · Tolled · COMMON. Collects unpaid hours. Hunting Imre. Has a clockwork jaw.
- **Pell & Pim — The Bellwright Twins** · Artificer · Villainous · — · COMMON. Brask's saboteurs. Finish each other's sentences and each other's work. One card, two people.

**THE MARGIN**
- **Mother Ink — Namethief** · Trickster · Villainous · Unsung · RARE. Tattooist who writes names on the nameless — names she steals from the living. The name on her own arm keeps changing.
- **Dask Holloway — Remnant Knight** · Sentinel · Heroic · Knight, Unsung · COMMON. The twelfth city's only surviving knight, because he was outside the walls. Remembers nothing but the oath. *Bond: the Hollow King.*
- **Grael — Scavenger-Prophet** · Seer · Corrupted · Unsung · COMMON. Half-unsung. His prophecies come out with words missing. Some of them have come true anyway.

**THE VIGIL**
- **High Cantor Absolom Reyne** · Duelist · Corrupted · Sleepless · EPIC. Hero of the Unended Night, when he kept Lanternfast singing for nine days without sleep. Hasn't slept since. His voice is ruined; his faith is not. Wants to burn the Margin.
- **Maren Solt — Hush-Hunter** · Duelist · Rogue · Sleepless · RARE. Hunts the things that walk in the Hush. Paid by the Vigil, loyal to nobody. Found Lirae second, and decided on the spot that nobody was taking her anywhere. *Bond: Lirae.*
- **Brother Anselm — The Sleepless** · Martyr · Heroic · Sleepless · COMMON. Hasn't slept in eleven years. Sees things in his peripheral vision. Is still gentle.
- **Ione — Choir-Child** · Artificer · Heroic · Child · COMMON. Nine. Sings the midnight verse at Lanternfast. Her note holds longer than anyone's. It's starting to scare the Cantors.

## 5. Relationship rules

- **Bonds are printed.** Any competitive effect from a relationship is written on the card as `BOND — Name: …`.
- **Cosmetic relationships are unlimited.** Special intro lines, shared Vault pages and achievements (e.g. *"Unfinished Business: win a Clash with Aurelle against the Hollow King"*) carry no competitive effect.
- **The Aurelle / Hollow King meeting** is a cosmetic-only special intro at launch: when they face each other, the music drops out for one beat and both portraits flicker. No explanation is given. That's the first public breadcrumb of the biggest secret in the game.
