## 2. The Firstfall Claims (season-long faction event)

- **Pledge.** In week 1 every player pledges to one of the five factions. It's free and permanent for the season, and gives a pledge frame plus a faction title track.
- **Claim points.** Win any Match with **4+ Champions of your pledged faction** in your Octave → +1 Claim point for that faction. Final Clash wins → +2. Daily Read wins → +1.
- **Weekly standings** (shown in the Hall of Coda on the home screen). The weekly leader's faction gets **+10% event tokens** for its pledges the next week. No gameplay power.
- **Pledge track** (free): faction card-back → faction Vault banner → faction Alt Art Common → faction title *"Claimant of the [Faction]"* → faction Legendary Alt Art at the final tier.
- **Final standings pick the epilogue** (§5, Chapter X). The winning faction *holds custody of Lirae* in the canonical opening of Season 2. Every epilogue leads to the same Season 2 story; custody changes who is holding her and why.

## 3. Story chapters

Each chapter is a 60–90 s illustrated scene (portrait panels, one line per panel), a **Story Clash**, and a closing panel. The Story Clash uses scripted Octaves and teaches or tests one thing.

### Chapter I — The Fall *(tutorial: Champion → Order → Reveal)*
**Setting:** the rampart of Cinderhold at midnight.

> TAMSIN: *"Sir Hadric. Sir Hadric, the sky—"*
> HADRIC: *"I see it, girl."*
> *(A star moves. Stars do not move.)*
> HADRIC: *"Get the Regent."*
> *(Across the Meridian: an Arbiter in Coda sets down her quill. A clockmaker in Carillon stops every clock in the room. A child in Lanternfast stops singing for the first time in her life. In the Margin, a man with half a face looks up and smiles.)*

**Story Clash:** Tamsin & Hadric vs *a Hush-shape* (tutorial bot). You learn to deploy into lanes, then give Orders. **Lesson:** Strike › Scheme › Guard › Strike, and what each one hits.
**Closing panel:** the star hits the Blank. The fog *rings*, like a struck bell.

### Chapter II — Into the Blank *(Octave; the Margin)*
Vega Sorn reaches the crater first, with Nyx behind her. Something is sitting in the centre of the crater, humming. Vega writes in her book: *NYX. NYX. NYX. And a girl made of light.*
> VEGA: *"Don't touch her."*
> NYX: *"I wasn't going to touch her. I was going to take her."*
> LIRAE *(eyes closed)*: *"You're both afraid. You're afraid in the same key."*

**Story Clash:** your first real Octave vs Maren Solt's hunters, who arrive second. **Lesson:** lanes and Voice: an empty lane lets their Champion hit your Voice, and a fallen Champion doesn't come back.

### Chapter III — The Claims *(Focus)*
At Coda, the First Silence rings a clapperless bell once. Oriel Dane speaks her one word per year: **"CLAIMS."** Under the Accord, anything that falls from the sky belongs to whoever wins it by Clash. Five factions file. Aurelle and Caska see each other across the Stillhouse floor for the first time in twenty-one years. Neither speaks.

**Story Clash:** Crown vs Quiet exhibition. **Lesson:** Focus (+2): when to spend it, and when to hold it.

### Chapter IV — First Blood of the Accord *(READ)*
Corvin doesn't file a Claim. He rides into the Margin at night with the Cinder Widow and burns a scaffold-town, looking for the girl. It's the first act of war in nineteen years. Dask Holloway holds the bridge alone until the Hollow King arrives. The Hollow King lets Corvin leave. *"Tell your sister,"* he says, *"that I know what her crown weighs."*

**Story Clash:** Dask vs Corvin. The scripted Corvin **always Strikes once either Voice is low** (12 or less). **Lesson:** READ. Your first READ lands. *"I knew you were going to do that."*

### Chapter V — Iver's Choice *(the Quiet; Initiative)*
The Quiet summon Silencers to punish the Accord-breaker. Iver Hale, once a Crown knight, is chosen to bring in the Regent's own brother. Hadric rides to Coda to stop him and speaks Iver's name for the first time in nineteen years.
> HADRIC: *"Iver."*
> IVER: *"You remembered it."*
> HADRIC: *"I never forgot it. I stopped saying it."*

**Story Clash:** Hadric vs Iver: master against student, both Guard-heavy. **Lesson:** Shields and Initiative.

### Chapter VI — The Singing *(the Vigil)*
Lirae begins to hum in the Vigil's camp. Everyone within earshot starts *remembering things that didn't happen to them*. Ione's midnight note holds by itself for eleven minutes. High Cantor Absolom declares Lirae the Voice's answer and orders her carried to Lanternfast *"so the whole world can hear her"*. Caska refuses. Absolom has her stripped of her lantern.

**Story Clash:** Caska (no lantern: a scripted Focus-less Octave) vs Sister Morrow. **Lesson:** winning without resources.

### Chapter VII — The Empty Portrait *(the Crown)*
In Cinderhold, Aurelle stands before the empty frame in the Vantaine gallery. For the first time, she sees a face in it for one heartbeat: a man, older than her, with her mother's eyes. It's gone. She orders the frame burned. Old Nan refuses to do it. Lysander Crane quietly reports it to Corvin.

**Story Clash:** Aurelle vs *her own memory* (a mirror Octave of the player's). **Lesson:** reading yourself; the AI copies your habits from this season.

### Chapter VIII — The Hush Lengthens *(the Hourwrights)*
In Carillon, Ottavio Brask buys up every tolled hour in the city. Imre learns why: his father is building something that runs on *time*, and needs a great deal of it. Imre and the Unwound break into the counting house and find blueprints for a tuning fork with **three** tines. One of them is drawn in a different hand: his mother's.
> IMRE: *"You kept her drawings."*
> BRASK: *"I kept everything of hers, boy. That is what love is."*

**Story Clash:** Imre vs the Tollmaster. **Lesson:** Focus economy (the enemy steals Focus).

### Chapter IX — SOMETHING HAS ARRIVED *(World Boss)*
At midnight, the Hush doesn't end. It lasts forty-one minutes, then forty-two, then it doesn't stop at all. Something walks out of the crater where Lirae fell: a tall absence, shaped like a person, made of the silence between notes. The Vigil call it **The Rest**. Every faction's Champions fight side by side for the first time since the war.

**Story Clash:** the player's Octave vs The Rest (Phase 1, solo). Then the global boss opens.

### Chapter X — Firstfall *(finale)*
The Rest falls: the world killed it. In the silence afterward, Lirae opens her eyes for the first time. She walks to the edge of the Blank, where Aurelle is standing alone, and past her to the Hollow King, and she says something no one hears.

The **epilogue** is chosen by the Claims (§5).

**Final panel (all versions):** Aurelle looks at the Hollow King. The Hollow King looks at Aurelle. Both of them are crying and neither of them knows why.

## 4. World Boss: THE REST

**Announcement:** the home screen drains to black, all audio drops out, and the text **SOMETHING HAS ARRIVED** appears letter by letter with no sound.

**Global health:** 2,500,000 Resolve × active-player multiplier (tuned so the world kills it in about 60 hours).

**The fight:** a Match against the Rest's Octave with **boss rules**:

| Phase (by global HP) | Boss rule | Boss Octave |
|---|---|---|
| **I. The Silence Spreads** (100–66%) | Nobody has a READ at match start; **make an enemy Champion fall to earn your READ** | Hushborn ×6, The Quiet Thing, Mouth of the Rest |
| **II. Unsinging** (66–33%) | On each boss Reveal, **the Champion opposite can't use its highest printed Order** | + Unsung Shade, Echo of Solenne |
| **III. The Rest Is Written** (33–0%) | The boss **always holds Initiative**, and its Voice is 45; damage you deal while your own Voice is 10 or less counts triple | + The Stopped Choir, **The Rest (Heart)** |

**Damage:** all Voice and health damage you deal across the match, plus a 25% bonus if you silence the Rest. Personal, Alliance and global bars update live.

**Boss Champions** (not collectible; boss rules only):

| Boss Champion | Strike/Guard/Scheme/Health | Ability (kit DSL) |
|---|---|---|
| Hushborn | 5/4/5/6 | READs against this Champion always miss |
| The Quiet Thing | 6/6/3/8 | On Reveal: your opponent can't use Focus this round |
| Mouth of the Rest | 4/5/6/7 | +3 Power if your opponent READs this round |
| Unsung Shade | 6/3/6/6 | On Reveal: the enemy opposite can't use its highest printed Order |
| Echo of Solenne | 7/4/4/7 | +1 Power for each round in its winning streak (max +3) |
| The Stopped Choir | 4/4/5/5 | Last Words: the boss's other Champions gain +1 all stats |
| **The Rest (Heart)** | 8/8/8/14 | On Reveal: silence the enemy opposite. Every round end: give itself a 3 Shield |

**Rewards**

| Alliance | top 10% → Collector Packs for all contributors; top 1% → Alliance banner *The Rest Is Broken* |
