## Rules for every image
- **Style:** painterly dark-fantasy oil painting. Deep chiaroscuro, rim light, visible brushwork, fine painted grain. Faint concentric sound-ripples move through the air from the main light (the world is made of sound).
- **No text of any kind:** no letters, numbers, logos, signatures, watermarks, UI or frames.
- **Format:** PNG, exact size given per section. Keep file names exactly as written (case-sensitive).
- **Game UI space:** screen backgrounds sit behind game UI, so keep them calm and dark, with no single subject fighting for attention. Keep the top 12% and bottom 25% especially dark and plain.
- **Faction looks:**
  - **Crown:** obsidian glass, bone-white cloth, ember light, falling ash.
  - **Quiet:** black stone, cold silver, clapperless bells, moonlight.
  - **Hourwrights:** verdigris brass, clockwork, amber gas-light, bell-jars of glowing hours.
  - **Margin:** scaffolds over white fog, violet lamps, rags, half-erased people.
  - **Vigil:** white and gold, candles, lanterns, sleepless eyes, choirs.
