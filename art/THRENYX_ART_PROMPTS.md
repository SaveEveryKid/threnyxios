# THRENYX — Art Production Prompts (Firstfall)

> **Superseded** by `art/THRENYX_ART_MASTER.md` (every image the game needs, generated by `tools/artpack.mjs`). Kept because the master pack reuses its hand-written briefs.

Feed these prompts to the image agent **one at a time, in order**. Every prompt is self-contained, so no prompt depends on the agent remembering an earlier one.

---

## 0. Instructions for the image agent (paste this first, once)

```
You are producing the complete art set for THRENYX, a premium dark-fantasy digital trading card game. The quality bar is higher than the best trading card game art ever printed: museum-grade painted illustration, not "game ad" art.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE
- Generate exactly ONE image per prompt, at the size stated in the prompt (default 1024x1536, portrait).
- NO text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI, card borders or frames inside the art, unless the prompt explicitly asks for a frame.
- Save each image as PNG with the exact filename given in the prompt, inside the folder given.
- If a result looks generic, plastic, over-smoothed or "AI-looking", has wrong anatomy (hands, eyes, extra fingers), or doesn't match the character description, regenerate it before moving on.
- When a prompt says CUTOUT, also save a second PNG of the SAME image with the background removed (transparent), named as instructed. Do not regenerate the cutout; cut it out of the approved image.
- After the final prompt, package everything into threnyx-art.zip using this folder structure:

threnyx-art/
  00_style/        style anchor
  cards/           30 Champion card artworks
  immortal/        Immortal masterpiece variants (+ cutouts)
  cutouts/         transparent Champion cutouts for Mythic cards
  frames/          6 rarity frames (transparent centers)
  cardback/        card back
  packs/           pack wrappers
  scenes/          app backgrounds and key art
```

## The shared style (built into every prompt below)

**THRENYX STYLE:** painterly dark-fantasy illustration with the finish of a hand-painted oil on canvas. Visible, confident brushwork in backgrounds, and tight rendering on faces, hands and hero details. Cinematic chiaroscuro: most of the frame falls into deep shadow, and light is rare and precious. A strong rim light separates the character from the background. Every character has a memorable silhouette readable as a black shape. Faces are specific, adult and characterful, never generic or doll-like. Materials are tactile: burned iron, lacquer, verdigris brass, candle wax, paper, ink. Painted grain throughout.

**WORLD MOTIF (in every image):** Threnyx is a world made of sound. Somewhere in each image, faint concentric ripples move through the air like visible sound waves radiating from the main light source. They should be subtle, not a special effect.

**CARD COMPOSITION (all card art):** portrait 1024x1536. The final card crops to 5:7, removing about 50px from the top and bottom, so keep crowns and weapon tips at least 90px from the top edge. The focal point (face) sits in the upper 55% of the frame. The lower 35% will be covered by the card name and rules text, so keep it darker and simpler (clothing, mist, ground) with no important detail there.
