# THRENYX — MASTER ART BRIEF (Season I: Firstfall)

> **How to use:** give this entire file to the image agent as ONE prompt. It contains every image the game needs (205 images to generate + 10 cutouts derived from them = 215 files) and ends with a single zip, `threnyx-art.zip`.
> Generated by `game/tools/artpack.mjs` from the game's card data, so names, factions and rarities match the game exactly. Unzip into `game/client/art/` and the game loads it through `manifest.json`.

---

## 0. AGENT CONTRACT — follow exactly

```
You are the art department for THRENYX, a premium dark-fantasy digital trading card game. Your job is to produce EVERY image listed in section 4 of this document, then package them into one zip. The quality bar is higher than the best trading card art ever printed: museum-grade painted illustration, never "game ad" art.

WORKFLOW (do not stop, do not ask questions, do not skip):
1. Work through the jobs in section 4 strictly in order, J001 to J205.
2. BUILD THE FULL PROMPT: every job's prompt contains tags in curly braces ({STYLE}, {LOOK:crown}, {CARD-COMP}, {MYTHIC-COMP}, {BANS}, {UI-BANS}). Before sending a prompt to the image model, replace each tag with its full text from section 1, word for word. Never send a prompt with an unexpanded tag, and never shorten a block.
3. For each job, generate exactly ONE image from the expanded prompt, at the exact pixel size given, as a PNG (24-bit colour; 32-bit with alpha only for cutouts).
4. Inspect the result against the QUALITY GATE below. If it fails, regenerate (up to 3 attempts per job). If all 3 fail, keep the best attempt, and record the job ID and the reason in QA_LOG.md. Then continue. Never stop the run.
5. Save the approved image at EXACTLY the path given in "SAVE AS", inside the root folder threnyx-art/. Paths and filenames are case-sensitive; use them character for character. Never rename, never add suffixes such as "_final" or "_v2".
6. If a job lists DERIVED FILES, create them from the approved image of THAT job (never regenerate): remove the background to full transparency and keep only the named figure (with its crown, weapon, and the glow immediately around it). The derived file keeps the SAME pixel size and the SAME position as the source (it must overlay the source exactly), saved as a PNG with alpha.
7. Character consistency: J001 (style anchor) sets the look for everything. When a later job says CHARACTER CONSISTENCY, use the named approved image(s) as visual reference so the same character keeps the same face, age, build, costume and props.
8. After the last job, do the PACKAGING steps in section 5 and deliver threnyx-art.zip.

QUALITY GATE (every image):
- Matches the job's subject, faction look and composition rules.
- Painterly oil-painting finish with painted grain, deep shadows and a rim light. NOT glossy, plastic, over-smoothed, airbrushed, photographic, 3D-rendered, anime or cartoon.
- Anatomy is correct: 5 fingers per hand, symmetrical eyes, natural limbs, no melted or duplicated features.
- NO text, letters, numbers, pseudo-writing, logos, watermarks, signatures, frames or borders anywhere in the image, unless the job explicitly asks for one. (No job in this document asks for one.)
- Card art (1024x1536): the face sits between 12% and 50% of the height; the top 90px and the top-left and top-right 160px corners hold no faces; the lower 45% is dark and simple.
- Exact pixel dimensions. Full bleed (no letterboxing).

DO NOT:
- merge jobs, split jobs, or produce variations beyond one approved image per job;
- invent extra characters' names, add lettering, add the game's UI, or add a card frame;
- change any filename or folder name;
- stop to ask for approval. The style anchor is approved by default.
```

## 1. Canonical blocks (expand these tags, word for word, into every prompt)

**{STYLE}**
```
Painterly dark-fantasy illustration with the finish of a hand-painted oil on canvas: museum-grade, visible confident brushwork in the background, tight rendering on faces, hands and hero details. Cinematic chiaroscuro: most of the frame falls into deep shadow; light is rare and precious. A strong rim light separates the subject from the background. The silhouette must read as a clear black shape. Faces are specific, adult (unless a child is described) and characterful, never generic, doll-like or glossy. Tactile materials (burned iron, black glass, lacquer, verdigris brass, candle wax, paper, ink). Fine painted grain over the whole image. World motif: Threnyx is a world made of sound, so somewhere in the image faint concentric ripples move through the air like visible sound waves radiating from the main light source; subtle, never a special effect.
```

**{LOOK:crown}**
```
THE ASHEN CROWN: black volcanic glass (obsidian) armour and architecture, bone-white cloth, ember-red linings, grey ash always falling like snow, the burned imperial ruins of Solenne and the black-glass fortress of Cinderhold. Palette: ash grey, bone white, obsidian black, ember orange.
```

**{LOOK:quiet}**
```
THE QUIET: austere black stone and cold silver; clapperless bells worn on cords (bells with no tongue inside); vows of silence (sealed lips, linen over the mouth, wax seals); the silent city of Coda on the still lake called the Stillwater; geometric architecture, moonlight. Palette: black, cold silver, white, moon blue.
```

**{LOOK:hour}**
```
THE HOURWRIGHTS: the canal-city of Carillon: verdigris copper, polished brass, clockwork, gears and escapements, gas-lamps, bell-towers, steam; stolen "tolled hours" stored as glowing amber light in bell-jars and vials. Palette: verdigris green, brass gold, amber, soot black.
```

**{LOOK:margin}**
```
THE MARGIN: the Blank, a fog-field where the world is half-remembered: paper-white fog, ink-black shadows, violet lamps; rickety scaffold-towns (Scrawl) built over the fog; things half-drawn, with unfinished ink lines, and figures partly erased into blank paper. Palette: paper white, ink black, violet.
```

**{LOOK:vigil}**
```
THE VIGIL: Lanternfast, the city of lamps: midnight-indigo nights, candle-gold light, lanterns, censers and choirs, white-and-gold vestments, pilgrims who never sleep (bright, hollow, wakeful eyes). Palette: midnight indigo, candle gold, warm white.
```

**{CARD-COMP}**
```
Portrait 1024x1536. The game crops the art to 5:7 (removing about 50px at the top and bottom) and lays its own frame, name and stats over it, so:
- The focal point (the face) sits between 12% and 50% of the image height, horizontally near the centre.
- Keep 90px clear at the very top (no crown or weapon tips touching the edge) and keep the top-left and top-right 160x160px corners free of faces (badges sit there).
- The lower 45% will be covered by the name and stats: keep it darker and simpler (clothing, mist, ground). No important detail, face or hand there.
- Full bleed: the painting fills the whole canvas with no border, vignette frame or letterboxing.
```

**{MYTHIC-COMP}**
```
CUTOUT-READY: the figure must be cleanly separable from the background (a clear rim-lit silhouette with no background element crossing in front of it), because a transparent cutout will be derived from this exact image. The head or crown may rise to within 60px of the top edge (the game lets Mythic figures break out of the frame), never touching it.
```

**{BANS}**
```
FORBIDDEN: any text, letters, numbers, runes that look like writing, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI, card frames, borders, stat boxes or panels. No modern objects, no firearms, no photographic or 3D-render look, no anime or cartoon style, no resemblance to existing franchises or real people. No extra fingers or limbs, no distorted anatomy (scars and burns that the subject describes are fine).
```

**{UI-BANS}**
```
FORBIDDEN: any text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, signatures, buttons or UI drawn into the image, frames, borders. No modern objects, no photographic or 3D-render look, no anime or cartoon style.
```

## 2. Rarity presence (already written into each card prompt)

- COMMON: an intimate, grounded moment; simple staging; one main light source.
- RARE: a confident character portrait with one striking, memorable detail.
- EPIC: dramatic staging, a strong secondary light, a richer environment.
- LEGENDARY: iconic, heroic staging in a grand environment; the character's defining moment; a subtle gold warmth in the light.
- MYTHIC: the most spectacular image scale in the set; the character dominates the frame; god-rays and visible sound-rings; awe.

## 3. Card composition (why the safe zones exist)

```
1024 x 1536 card art (game crops to 5:7 and draws its frame, name and stats on top)
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐ 0
│▒▒▒▒ badge │   keep 90px clear   │ badge ▒▒▒▒│ 90px   (corners: no faces)
│                                        │
│            FACE / FOCAL POINT          │ 12% - 50%
│                                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────┤ 55%
│   name + stats + rules text overlay    │
│   (dark, simple: robes, mist, ground)  │ lower 45%
└────────────────────────────────────────┘ 1536
```

**Mythic cards** also get a transparent cutout of the figure, which the game lifts in front of the card frame for a 3D "breakout" effect and uses for phone-tilt parallax. Mythic figures therefore need a clean, rim-lit silhouette.
